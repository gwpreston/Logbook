# Phase 40.1 — Issues log

*The fault you've noticed and haven't fixed yet.*

Status: 🚧 in progress · ships with [Phase 40.2](phase-40.2.md) as
**v3.6.0** · file lives in `docs/phases/`

Logbook records what was done to a car (service records), what happened
to it (incidents) and what is due (schedules, documents, reminders). It
has nowhere for **a fault you've noticed and haven't fixed**: "knock from
front left under braking", "slow leak, rear right", "advisory: brake pipes
corroded". Today these end up as manual reminders, which need a due date
the owner doesn't have, or in a note that nothing reads.

An **issue** has a date, the mileage, a description and a status
(*open*, *watching*, *fixed*). It closes by linking to the service record
that fixed it. Open issues appear in *Needs attention*. Two things already
in Logbook feed it: the *recommended work* lines Phase 26.4 reads from
invoices ([Phase 40.2](phase-40.2.md)), and, from [Phase 41](phase-41.md),
MOT advisories.

Phase 40 was planned as one and split in two (decided 2026-10-08, #318):
this part builds issues and everything on Logbook's own pages; 40.2 takes
them to the recommended-work card, the API, Ask, MCP, CSV and backups, and
releases.

**No AI diagnosis** (decided 2026-10-08 by the owner). Guessing the cause
of a fault is the one suggestion where a confident wrong answer could hurt
someone, and it doesn't fit Logbook's grounded-only approach. Logbook
records the owner's words and links the fix; it never suggests what the
fault is.

Read [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md) and [`spec.md`](../../spec.md) §7.37
first, then §6 (Issue, IssueFix, IssueUpdate, OdometerReading, Reminder,
Attachment, Entry authorship), §7.4, §7.6, §7.10, §7.12, §7.16, §7.19,
§7.21, §7.24 and §7.29 (the incident pages are the nearest pattern).

**Prerequisites:** [Phase 38](phase-38.md) complete and green. Phases
[39.1](phase-39.1.md) to [39.3](phase-39.3.md) are built (v3.5.0), so
[Phase 40.2](phase-40.2.md) gives issues their edit, delete, attachment
and webhook endpoints.

---

## Goals

1. **Issues:** add, edit, delete, with status, *Affects safety*, updates
   over time and attached photos or PDFs.
2. **Fixing:** a service record can fix one or more issues, from either
   side, or an issue is fixed without a record; the issue shows what fixed
   it.
3. ***Needs attention*** shows open issues, and watching ones once their
   look-again point passes; the look-again point raises a reminder.
4. The Issues tab, overview card, issue page, fleet page, History, print
   and the sale pack; the demo seed.

## Not in scope

- **AI diagnosis** in any form: no "possible causes", no Ask tool that
  suggests one, no AI insight about a fault's cause.
- The recommended-work card, API, webhooks, Ask, MCP and CSV:
  [Phase 40.2](phase-40.2.md).
- **MOT advisories** as a source: [Phase 41](phase-41.md), which writes
  through this phase's create path.
- A **severity** or safety rating set by Logbook. The owner's words are
  the record; *Affects safety* is the owner's tick (#310).
- Costing an issue (estimates live in quotes and, once paid, in the
  service record).

## Spec

[`spec.md`](../../spec.md) §7.37 *Issues* is the whole of it, with §6
Issue, IssueFix and IssueUpdate. In short:

- **Statuses:** *open*, *watching* (optional look-again date and/or
  mileage), *fixed* (linked to record(s), or without one with a note,
  #308). Unlinking the last record returns the issue to its earlier
  status; *It's back* reopens and keeps the links.
- **Odometer:** an issue's or an update's mileage adds a reading (source
  `issue`, #307).
- ***Affects safety*** (#310): the owner's tick; first in lists and in
  *Needs attention*, in words and the danger colour.
- **Look-again reminder** (#311): source `issue`, generated by *Sync*,
  *Done* = *Looked at it*; needs `reminders`.
- **Pages:** Issues tab after Maintenance (#309), overview card, issue
  page with timeline, fleet `/issues`, add/edit modal and page, chooser
  entry; *Fixes* checklist on the maintenance form.
- ***Needs attention***: *Open issue* (*Log the repair*, *Watch*) and
  *Look again* (*Log the repair*, *Watch again*, *Reopen*, #315), no
  *Hide*.
- **Updates** with a note are editable and deletable under `EntryAccess`;
  automatic status changes are not (#316).
- **History** kinds *Issue noticed* and *Issue fixed*, *Issues* chip;
  print includes fixed issues; sale pack *Include open issues*, off by
  default (#312).
- **Module** `issues`, on by default, `FEATURES_ISSUES`.

## Decisions (and why)

- **The owner's words, not a diagnosis.** A wrong guess about brakes or
  steering is dangerous in a way a wrong fuel figure isn't. Logbook keeps
  what was noticed, when, at what mileage, and what fixed it. That is
  also exactly what a mechanic or a buyer wants.
- **Fixed means linked, normally.** The value of the log is the trail from
  symptom to repair, so the record is offered first; *Fixed without a
  record* exists because some faults just stop, and an invented record
  would be worse data (#308).
- **Watching is a status, not a hidden item.** A deliberate decision to
  live with an advisory deserves to be recorded, and the look-again point
  brings it back, in *Needs attention* and as a reminder that reaches the
  owner's channels (#311).
- ***Now*, not *Check*.** *Check* means "the data may be wrong". An open
  fault is real work, like an overdue service.
- **A tab and a card** (#309): issues are a running log the owner returns
  to, so they get a tab like Incidents; the card keeps open ones in view.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A fault can be logged with date, mileage, description and files, kept
   up to date, watched, and closed by the record that fixed it (or
   without one).
2. Open issues are in *Needs attention*; watching ones return when their
   look-again point passes, and their reminder reaches the owner.
3. Nowhere does Logbook suggest what a fault is.
4. Definition of done (CLAUDE.md §11) holds.
